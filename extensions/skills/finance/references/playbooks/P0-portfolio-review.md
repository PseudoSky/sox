# P0 portfolio-review

Stage: portfolio. Owns: holdings and exposure reads. Reads: an inlet record (`.finance/accounts/<id>/current.json`). Emits: analysis receipt `.finance/analysis/<id>-<as_of>.json`.

1. Import: follow SKILL.md "Inlet". Import once per file; the core refuses a re-import of the same file.
2. Analyze: `analyze --account <id> --write`. Consolidated view: `analyze --consolidated`. Currencies are never converted; mixed currencies are reported separately.
   Fund look-through: `analyze --account <id> --lookthrough [--top N] [--write]`, or `--consolidated --lookthrough` for the combined exposure per currency. Each ETF or mutual fund is replaced by its freshest holdings: the issuer's own daily file via TickerInside (CC BY 4.0, keyless; `funds.<sym>.attribution` carries the credit line) when it covers the fund from an issuer file, else the latest SEC N-PORT filing (`--sec-only` skips TickerInside; mutual funds always use N-PORT) (weights applied to the position's value), so every row shows the underlying share of equity and the funds and accounts it comes through. `--top N` counts non-cash rows (cash is listed apart under `cash`); `merged_share_classes` totals issuers listed under several tickers (GOOGL plus GOOG); each fund's `weight_sum` shows how far its filing is from 100%. Tested on 36 registered ETFs and mutual funds (broad, international, muni, aggregate, high-yield, Treasury, leveraged, option-income): bond and other non-equity holdings are grouped by issuer (`DBT:<issuer>`), never ticker-mapped; a symbol with no registered-fund filing (GLD, IBIT, stocks) stays one line and does not make the run `incomplete`. Money-market funds count as cash, options are not decomposed, funds-of-funds are not looked through. Weights are as of each fund's `period_end` (`layer` says issuer-file or sec-nport; issuer files are cached two days, filings a month) (a warning appears past 120 days); the first run for a new fund takes minutes (ticker lookups are rate-limited) and later runs read the cache under `.finance/funds/`. The SEC requires a contact: set `FINANCE_SEC_CONTACT` to your email.
3. Report the receipt path, equity, allocation, cash %, top concentration, and the warnings verbatim.

Ungated. Analysis is the snapshot plus paper-ledger events newer than the import, so paper fills show in equity and positions. Money is rounded to cents; percentages to 2 dp. `cash_pct` = cash plus `cash_equivalent` positions, as a brokerage cash balance includes its sweep fund.
`unrealized.pnl_pct` is measured on the cost of non-cash positions only, so it differs from a broker figure that includes the sweep fund in its cost base; the receipt labels its basis. An export taken during the session is valued at its intraday prices and `price_as_of` keeps only the date; check the export's own time line and say so. `open_risk` is the summed risk of active stored plans (0 when none exist), not a volatility or drawdown measure; this skill's risk view is concentration, cash and open plan risk.
