# SIFT-CLICK-REFLECT Process — Full Iteration Log

## Initial Process Design (after synthesizing 6 frameworks)

| Phase | Name | Action |
|-------|------|--------|
| 0 | Precision Search | Generate/test/refine search terms. Use quotes, site:, exclude. |
| 1 | Click Restraint | Scan full SERP. Note domains, dates, snippets. Select 3-5 candidates. Do NOT click yet. |
| 2 | Lateral Triage | Open tabs. Search each source/author as a whole. What do OTHERS say? Grade A–E. |
| 3 | Deep Read | CRAAP: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose. |
| 4 | Trace | Trace claims to original context. Distinguish source vs inference. |
| 5 | Audit | Record search, source, what was read vs inferred. Grade confidence. |
